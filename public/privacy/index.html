Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026. Color Context is designed so your documents and annotations stay on your device.

What is stored

Files you open, labels you add, display preferences, and recent-workspace metadata are stored in your browser using IndexedDB and local storage. They are not sent to us.

Exports

Nothing is shared unless you explicitly download an annotated PNG or workspace JSON and choose where to send it. A workspace export includes the original source file and its annotations.

Network activity

The app loads its own static files from color-context.sociobot.in. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, remote fonts, or third-party runtime scripts.

Deleting data

Use “Remove file” inside a workspace to delete that file and its labels. Clearing this site’s storage in your browser removes all saved workspaces and preferences.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the project repository linked by the Param Factory.
